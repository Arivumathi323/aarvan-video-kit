# Filming card · Aarvan Labs

Read this before you hit record. How you film decides how good the edit can be: the editor can cut and decorate, but it can't fix a dark face, a quiet mic or a head touching the top of the frame.

## 1 · Set up (2 minutes)

- **Phone on something steady:** a tripod, books, or a shelf. The shot never moves.
- **Shape:** **vertical** for Reels and Shorts, **landscape** for YouTube, assignments and clients. Decide before you start.
- **Quality:** back camera, **1080p or 4K, 30 fps**. Laptop webcams are 720p, so avoid them for anything you'll post.
- **Framing:** chest or waist up, eyes about a third of the way down, and **space above your head**. That space is where the Aarvan Drop word lands and where labels go. If your head touches the top of the frame, there's no room for either.
- **Light in front of you:** face a window or a lamp. **Never sit with a window or a bright wall behind you**, or your face goes dark.
- **Sound:** a quiet room with the fan and AC off and windows closed. Keep the phone within an arm's length, or use a clip-on mic. Your voice is the most important part of the video.

## 2 · Test shot (30 seconds)

Record 5–10 seconds of yourself talking normally and send it to Claude: **"check my test shot"**. It runs `aarvan testshot`, which checks orientation, quality, headroom, distance, light, steadiness, voice level and room noise, and tells you exactly what to fix. Fix it, then film.

## 3 · While you talk

- **Hook first.** Your first sentence is the most interesting thing you'll say.
- **Pause for half a second between points.** Clean pauses make clean cuts.
- **Mistakes are fine.** Don't stop. Pause, then say the whole sentence again. The editor keeps your last clean take.
- **Say your big word clearly, and pause after it.** That's where the Aarvan Drop goes.
- **Say the count first:** "three things", "my projects are…". The editor turns it into a list on screen.
- **Name real things:** n8n, XSPEED, Linux. Labels come from the words you say.
- **Say numbers clearly,** and know where they come from. Every number on screen should be true.
- **End cleanly:** your last line, then stay still for 2 seconds.

## 4 · Send it

Send the raw video in a new chat with one line saying where it's going: *"Edit this for Instagram Reels"*, *"for my assignment"* or *"for YouTube"*.
